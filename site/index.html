An add-on for Anki

Your spreadsheet is your deck.

Write flashcards in Google Sheets, one row each. SheetCards brings them into Anki and keeps them in step whenever the sheet changes.

Download.ankiaddon Try the preview

Free and open source · Anki 25.x or later on a computer

What it does

  • Your columns, any language

    Name columns Word, Nghĩa or 汉字. Each one becomes a field in Anki.

  • One row of settings

    Row 2 sets each column's side, size and colour — size=48; color=muted. No template editing.

  • Read aloud

    Add tts=en_US and Anki speaks the column with the voices on your device.

  • Type the answer

    type=nc puts a box on the card and checks your spelling — tone marks optional.

  • Cloze and handwriting

    Hide words with {{c1::…}}, or write Chinese characters stroke by stroke with draw.

  • See it before you sync

    The preview runs the add-on's own code in your browser, and can hand you an .apkg for your phone.

How it works

  1. Make a sheet

    Row 1 holds the column names: ID, then any columns you like. Each row below is a card.

  2. Share it

    In Google Sheets: Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer, then copy the link.

  3. Connect it

    In Anki: Tools → SheetCards → Connect a Google Sheet… and paste the link. After that, Ctrl+Shift+S brings in whatever changed.

Install

  1. Download sheetcards.ankiaddon.
  2. In Anki, open Tools → Add-ons, press Install from file… and pick that file.
  3. Restart Anki. A SheetCards menu appears under Tools.
